description=Local click segmentation: click an object on any imagery layer and get its outline as a polygon (SAM 2.1, the lighter EdgeTAM or SAM 3, on an NVIDIA GPU, Apple M-series or the CPU). No account, no cloud.
about=Detects the NVIDIA GPU and installs the matching CUDA build of PyTorch (about 4-5 GB), or Metal on Apple M-series; falls back to the CPU if the GPU cannot be used. Left-click to include, right-click to exclude, Enter to save. Hover preview, alternative outlines and refine options (expand, simplify, fill holes, square corners, smooth). On first use it installs its own engine (private Python + PyTorch), either online or without internet from an engine package file, picks NVIDIA CUDA, Apple Metal or CPU automatically, and then asks before downloading the model you pick (56 MB to 3.4 GB) from Hugging Face. Linux, Windows and macOS. Imagery never leaves the computer.
